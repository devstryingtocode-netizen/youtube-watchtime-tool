Why use YouTube Timestamp Link Generator?

Share a video that starts at a specific point to save time for friends, students and teammates. The link uses YouTube's standard start-time parameter.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does a YouTube timestamp link work?

A timestamp parameter asks the video player to start at a particular point in the video.

Do timestamped links work on mobile and in the YouTube app?

They use YouTube's standard video URL format; behavior may vary between apps and browsers.

Can I use a youtu.be short link?

Yes. The generated link is normalized to a standard YouTube watch URL.

How do I make an embedded video start at a specific time?

This generator creates a watch link. Embed players use a separate start parameter.

Why does my browser sometimes ignore the timestamp?

Player behavior can depend on the app, browser and URL format.

Can I create a link that starts and ends at specific times?

This tool sets a start time only.
