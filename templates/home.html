How to use YouTube Playlist Length

  1. Paste a playlist or video link

    Copy any YouTube playlist URL or video URL and paste it into the text box above.

  2. Set optional parameters

    Optionally limit the video range, set a custom playback speed, or enter how many videos you've already watched.

  3. Click Analyze Playlist

    See total duration at multiple speeds, plus progress tracking and a personalized watch schedule.

Why use YouTube Playlist Length?

Whether you're planning to binge a full YouTube course, estimate how long a podcast playlist will take, or track your progress through a lecture series — this free tool gives you accurate watch-time estimates in seconds. No sign-up, no ads blocker needed, no data stored. Just paste, click, and know exactly when you'll finish.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find the total length of a YouTube playlist?

Paste the playlist URL into the box above and click "Analyze Playlist". The tool shows the total duration and watch times at different speeds.

Can I see how long specific videos in a playlist will take?

Yes — use the Range Start and Range End fields to select a subset of videos (for example, videos 10–50).

Does this work with private playlists?

Private playlists are not accessible through the public YouTube Data API. Unlisted playlists may work when you have their direct link.

How accurate is the duration?

Duration values come from YouTube's API. Unavailable or deleted videos are excluded from duration totals and identified in the results.

Can I use my own YouTube API key?

Yes — enter your YouTube Data API v3 key in the optional field. The key is used only for the current server request and is not stored.

How does the Watch Schedule calculator work?

Enter your daily viewing time, select the days you watch and choose a playback speed. The schedule skips unselected days when estimating a finish date.
